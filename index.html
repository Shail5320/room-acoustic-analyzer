🎙️ Analyze Recording

ℹ️ How to record with two devices
  1. Play the test sweep from a second device (phone, laptop, speaker) at a fixed position and moderate volume.
  2. Turn OFF equalisers, spatial audio, and loudness enhancement on the playback device.
  3. Turn OFF noise reduction, voice isolation, and auto-gain (AGC) on the recording device.
  4. Start recording first, then play. Record the whole sweep plus at least 3 s of decay (record at least 15 s total).
  5. Save as WAV or lossless if possible. Compressed recordings (m4a, mp3) work but may be less accurate.

Physical Assumptions & Acoustic Limitations

  • Diffuse Sound Field: Sabine and Eyring formulas assume a fully diffuse reverberant field. Small furnished rooms exhibit specular reflections and flutter echoes that deviate from statistical theory.
  • Room Modes (< 250 Hz): Below ~250 Hz, acoustic decay is dominated by modal resonances (standing waves) rather than statistical reverberation. Low-frequency absorption requires tuned membrane or Helmholtz bass traps, not thin porous panels.
  • Absorption is NOT Sound Insulation: Acoustic panels control internal room reflections, reverberation, and speech clarity. They do NOT prevent sound from escaping into adjacent rooms or block outside noise.
  • Placeholder Coefficients: All material coefficients in presets are placeholders. Verify manufacturer laboratory test reports (ISO 354 / ASTM C423) before purchasing materials.
